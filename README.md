# FMCG Personal Care: Regional Performance & Price Sensitivity Analysis

A data analysis and visualization project examining sales performance, marketing effectiveness, and consumer willingness-to-pay (WTP) across 17 provinces in Northern Thailand for personal care products (Shampoo, Facial Cleanser, and Body Wash).

---

## 📌 Context & Objectives

Evaluating FMCG performance purely through overall sales volume often hides key regional dynamics. Variations in purchasing power, population size, and marketing spend efficiency create uneven growth across territories.

This project was built to address three core business questions:
1. Marketing Efficiency: Which provinces yield the highest revenue return per dollar spent on marketing?
2. Price Sensitivity: At what price threshold does consumer demand drop significantly for each product category?
3. Market Prioritization: Where should trade marketing efforts be focused to optimize budget allocation?
